Questions and answers

What people ask most often about Tabi.

I have just arrived in a new city. Where should I go and what should I see?

Tabi shows the city’s special places and highlights those that match your interests. The sights of every city are grouped into themes, from the main landmarks to city life, so you can see what you have already seen and what is still ahead. Our aim is that you never miss a place you might love, and that every trip has a clear purpose.

Does Tabi take other travellers’ opinions into account? Coming soon

Yes. Travellers rate the places they have visited, and Tabi recommends what others have enjoyed.

I have travelled a lot but forgotten much of it. How can I remember where I have been?

Tabi recovers the places you have visited from your photos: from each picture it reads only the location and the date it was taken. After that your collection grows by itself, and every special place stays in it with the date of your visit. Trips used to be remembered through albums that had to be printed and labelled. Now the memory of them is always with you.

Can I add my own notes and photos to a place? Coming soon

Yes. You can add notes and your own photos to any place in your collection, and mark favourite places with a star to come back to them.

Can I add a place of my own that is not in Tabi? Coming soon

Yes. Mark a favourite place on the map and add a description and photos. Only you will see it, and you can share it with everyone if you wish.

I collect places: lighthouses, monasteries, national parks. How can Tabi help?

Tabi holds more than 60,000 special places on every continent. Choose what interests you, and the world map shows such places near you and in any country: mountains, deserts, lakes, waterfalls, islands, castles. Every place you reach is added to your collection.

How do I tell others where I have been when a photo proves nothing?

Say you have been to Everest, and you will most likely hear polite doubt. And how do you tell anyone about Point Nemo, the point of the ocean farthest from land? A photo of water explains nothing. Tabi counts a place only when you have really been there, and keeps where and when it happened. You can share the place card: it is both a story and a proof.

People ask me where I have been, and I cannot remember the details. What can I do?

Open Tabi: it shows the places you passed in a day and over a whole trip. That is an easy way to start a conversation.

Can I see on a map only the places I have been to? Coming soon

Yes. Your personal map holds only your places: all your geography on one screen.

How does Tabi count places?

By itself, in the background. Every place has a zone on the map. As soon as you are inside it, the place joins your collection, even with the app closed.

Are places I drive past counted?

No. Tabi counts the places you have visited, not those that flashed past a car or train window.

How is Tabi different from apps where you check in by hand?

There is nothing to check in: Tabi notices where you have been by itself. And it collects special places, not cafés and shops: castles, monasteries, national parks, islands, peaks.

Is Tabi free?

Yes, Tabi is free.

Does Tabi use much battery?

No. While your phone lies still, Tabi uses neither GPS nor network.

Does Tabi work without the internet?

Yes, fully: it counts places and shows the map and your collection. When you are back online, your collection is updated in your account.

What happens to my data?

Your route is kept on your phone and sent to your account once a day. Tabi does not take your photos: it reads only the location and date of each picture. You can export all your data and delete your account in the app settings.

Is there a version for iPhone?

Not yet: the first version is for Android.

When will Tabi be available?

The app is preparing for its release on Google Play. A link to the store will appear on this site.

Which languages does Tabi speak?

English, Czech, German, French, Spanish, Japanese and Russian.

How do I delete my account?

In the app settings. What happens to your data after deletion is described in the privacy policy.
