To use Tabi you need to sign in to an account: with email, Google or Facebook. You are responsible for what is done with your account. You can delete your account in the app settings (the “Delete account” row at the very end of Settings).

2a. Nickname, place suggestions and messages

Every account has a nickname for rankings: the app assigns a random one and you can change it. You can suggest a new place or city and write to the Tabi team. You may not use in your nickname, suggestions or messages insults, threats, harassment, calls to hatred or violence, obscene or sexually explicit content, advertising or spam, other people's personal data, impersonation of another person, or anything that breaks the law or infringes others' rights. The Tabi team reviews place suggestions before others can see them. We replace a nickname that breaks these rules with a random one, reject suggestions, and may block the account for repeated or serious violations. You can report an unacceptable nickname or content in the app (“Contact us”) or at support@tabimobile.com.

4. Ads, coins and subscription

Ads in Tabi are only short videos that you start yourself. Coins are Tabi's in-app unit: you get them for stamps at places you have visited, for collection milestones, for videos and from coin packs bought in Google Play. Coins for visits and coin spending are recorded on the phone at once, including offline, and reconciled with the server when online; we may take back coins obtained in breach of these terms. Coins have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money and cannot be transferred to other people. Neither coins nor the subscription can buy a place, city or country for your collection. Coin purchases are non-refundable, except where Google Play refunds them under its own rules or the law requires a refund. If Google Play refunds a coin pack or a subscription, the coins received for it are taken back in full; if your balance is lower, it becomes negative, and new coins first pay off this debt. While the balance is below zero or below the price, nothing can be opened or bought with coins. The Tabi Plus subscription removes videos and unlocks extra features; its price and contents are shown in the app before purchase. Google Play sells, renews and cancels the subscription under its own rules, including refunds; you can cancel at any time in Google Play, and the subscription stays active until the end of the paid period. You can also open subscription management from the app: Settings → “Tabi Plus subscription”, or the “Subscription” section at the end of the shop.

5. Fair use

Places are counted by the phone's real location. You may not fake your location, create accounts to get around limits, or interfere with the app or the server. We may cancel anything counted unfairly and block the account.

6. Safety on the way
