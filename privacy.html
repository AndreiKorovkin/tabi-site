Tabi Privacy Policy

Version of 18 November 2026 (first version: 27 September 2026).

Who we are

Tabi is a place-collection app for travellers. Developer: Andrei Korovkin. Contact: support@tabimobile.com.

Tabi consists of the Android app and the website tabimobile.com, which share one account. This policy has a common part and separate sections on the app and on the website: what is said about the app does not apply to the website, and what is said about the website does not apply to the app.

The Tabi app

This section applies only to the Tabi app for Android.

  • Location, including when the app is closed — to count the places, cities and countries you visit and to record the route you travel. Points are checked on the phone; route points, visit points and times are sent to our server.
  • Places of stay. From your route the phone itself finds places where you spend a long time or keep returning to (for example, home or your place of study), so that it can show them on the route map of the day together with the time spent in these places. They do not affect how your location is determined or how your route is recorded. Our server stores the centre and radius of such a zone and the name you give it, so that the zone appears on each of your phones and returns after the app is reinstalled. You can rename, move or delete a zone on the route map of the day by tapping its label; deleting your account erases all zones.
  • Photos and videos — location and time only. If you allow photo import, the app reads coordinates and dates from your photos and videos to mark the places you have visited, and keeps no more than one point per half hour within each area about a kilometre across to show the route of those days and its distance. These points, a location and a time without the photos or videos themselves, are stored on our server together with the rest of your route, so that the route appears on each of your phones and is restored after you sign in; undoing the photo import or deleting your account erases them. The photos and videos themselves never leave your phone.
  • Physical activity (still, walking, riding) — read on the phone itself for three things: to count only the places you visit on foot, to stop asking for location while you are still, and to show your distance per day. This data is never stored or sent to our servers.
  • Coins and subscription. Your coin balance, what coins were earned and spent on (the coin journal), unlocked countries, cities and items, and the subscription status. The journal is kept on the phone and reconciled with the server.
  • Device information. A random device id and device name (so one phone records the route), OS type.
  • Background diagnostics log (time, point, what the app decided) — to find and fix counting errors. Kept for 14 days.
  • Usage statistics. When you first and last opened Tabi, how often and how long you used it, which screens and windows you opened and which actions you took (for example, photo import, a video, a purchase), phone model, Android and Tabi versions, which permissions are on and the battery mode, and app errors. We collect it to improve Tabi: to understand what works and what gets in the way, and to help you if something is not counted. Detailed sessions are kept on the server for 14–21 days and then moved to our private archive; totals are kept while you have an account. Statistics are not shared with ad networks or third parties. You can turn them off in Tabi settings ("Send usage statistics"): sessions then stop being recorded and those already recorded are deleted.
  • The map on your phone. So that the map opens without repeated downloads, the app keeps downloaded place points, outlines and zones on your phone. This is Tabi's data, not your personal data; it is removed together with the app.

The website tabimobile.com

This section applies only to the website: the page for scanning media files on a computer and the account page.

  • Photos, videos and location history from a computer. The page tabimobile.com/computer scans files on your computer without an account: the browser reads in photos and videos only the shooting location and time and the camera model, and in a Google location history export and in Google Photos descriptions, as well as in route files (GPX, KML, TCX, GeoJSON), only places and times; the files themselves never leave the computer. The result of the scan is kept in this browser until you press “Start over”. If you save the result, your Tabi account receives the “place and time” points, the camera make and model, six characters of a fingerprint of its serial number, the names of the folders with the files and the name of the computer’s system and browser; they are kept until you delete the device in the app or delete your account. We email you the result with a picture of your route; emails with Tabi news only if you ticked this when saving. After every scan the server receives an anonymous summary without coordinates: the numbers of files and points, country codes, camera models, the reasons why files have no place, the language, the system and the browser. To limit requests, the server keeps a fingerprint of the connection address for no longer than two days. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to the site without cookies. The places, cities and countries found are added to your collection, and the points to your route, on the same terms as the points of photos and videos from your phone.
  • Points of the computer page before sign-in. Right after a scan, even without an account, the page sends to the Tabi server the same data as when saving: the “place and time” points, the camera make and model, six characters of a fingerprint of its serial number, the folder names and the name of the computer’s system and browser. They are stored under a random key that only this browser has; the server knows only a fingerprint of that key and does not link the points to a person. If you sign in to Tabi on this page, the points move to your account and the copy is erased; without sign-in the copy is erased after 30 days, and “Start over” erases it at once.
  • Signing in on the website. On tabimobile.com you can sign in to the same Tabi account as in the app (with Google or a code sent by email). The sign-in key stays in this browser until you choose “Sign out”. The account page shows your nickname, the subscription status, the phones with the app and the numbers of your countries, cities and special places: the website reads this information from the Tabi server when the page opens and keeps a copy only in this browser, for no longer than ten minutes.

Common to the app and the website

  • Account. Tabi can be used only with an account: sign in with email, Google or Facebook; your email address is stored. Your account has a nickname for rankings: the app assigns a random one and you can change it; other people see only the nickname and the number in rankings.
  • Emails. Tabi news is emailed only with your consent: a checkbox when you sign in or a switch in settings; you can unsubscribe in any such email or in settings. Service emails — sign-in codes, mandatory updates, account security, changes to the terms, a reminder that a subscription with renewal turned off ends soon — are sent without separate consent. Messages from the Tabi team are also shown in the app.
  • Messages to support. If you write to us in the app ("Contact us") or to support@tabimobile.com, we keep your messages, the Tabi team's replies, the request type and your email address, and with the first message from the app the Tabi version, phone model, Android version and app language. We need this to answer you and fix problems. Only Tabi developers and moderators read these messages; they are not shared with ad networks or third parties.

Ads and subscription

The free version of Tabi shows ads; a paid subscription removes ads and adds extra features. Payments for the subscription and coin packs are taken and processed by Google Play; we never see payment details. Our server receives purchase information from Google: the product, the order number, the subscription term and status, and refunds. So that Google can match a purchase to your account, Tabi gives Google an irreversible fingerprint (SHA-256) of your account number rather than the number itself. Ads in Tabi are only short videos that you start yourself to earn coins or to scan your photos; they never appear on their own. The videos are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). Google receives the device advertising ID, the IP address (and from it an approximate country and city), device information and how you watched the video; it uses them to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Your precise location, route, collection and photos are not sent to AdMob. To credit a reward, Google tells our server that the video was watched, together with your Tabi account number. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent to personalised ads before the first video; you can change it at any time in Tabi settings ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also turn off personalised ads in your phone settings (Google → Ads). More: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, do not share your location or route with ad networks, and do not track you across other apps.

Where data is stored

Database — Supabase, EU (Frankfurt). App website (tabimobile.com) — GitHub Pages. Sign-in code emails are sent by Resend (EU, Ireland), which receives only the email address and the message text. Emails to the support address are received, and replies to them as well as news and service emails sent, by the same Resend. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a program, is signing in. Google Play Integrity checks that the app and the phone are genuine (Google receives information about the device and the app). When you ask for a place description in your language, the translation is made by the Claude model of Anthropic (USA): it receives only the Wikipedia description text, the place name and the target language, without your personal data. Place descriptions come from our own copy of Wikipedia article introductions; if there is no copy for a place yet, the phone requests the description directly from Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation, USA), which receives the phone's IP address and the article title, but not your account or your location. Google sign-in is handled by Google, Facebook sign-in by Meta. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Retention

While you have an account, including messages to support: they are deleted with the account. Diagnostics log — 14 days, detailed usage sessions — up to 21 days on the server. Former anonymous accounts without any visit or route are deleted after 30 days of inactivity. Accounts blocked for violating the terms of use (threats, extortion, fraud) and the data linked to them are kept for four years after blocking as evidence to protect our rights, then deleted. Points sent by the computer page before sign-in are kept for 30 days unless you sign in earlier; “Start over” erases them at once.

Your rights

You can turn off location and photo access in phone settings at any time, sign out, and delete your account with all data (see below).

Children

Not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We will announce material changes to this policy in the app.

Account deletion

In the app: Settings → at the very end “Delete account”, then the code from the email. Special places in your collection, route, places of stay, countries, cities, photo marks, coins, devices, logs and the account itself are deleted. This cannot be undone.

Without the app: email support@tabimobile.com with the subject “Tabi account deletion” and your account email. We delete it within 30 days.
