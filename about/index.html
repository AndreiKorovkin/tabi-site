Tabi — a free app for collecting special places

Tabi gathers the special places you have visited into a personal collection and suggests new ones. An Android app in ten languages.

Special places

More than 75,000 places on every continent: castles and monasteries, national parks and islands, peaks, waterfalls, deserts and the main sights of cities. Not cafés or shops, but places worth setting out for.

Rarity

Every place has its own level of rarity: common, rare, epic, legendary or unique. There are only 53 unique places on Earth; among them is Point Nemo, the point of the ocean farthest from land. Travellers refine the levels of places with their own ratings.

A collection that grows by itself

Tabi works in the background and counts a place as soon as you are there. A city opens with the first place in it, a country as soon as you are on its territory. Tabi recovers the places of past trips from your photos.

Your memory

Mark your favourite places with a star to come back to them. A personal map with only the places you have visited is coming soon.

New places

The world map shows places that match your interests, near you and in any country. The sights of every city are grouped into themes, and every place comes with a story from Wikipedia in your language.

Sharing

You can share the card of any place with friends: it is both a story and a proof that you were there.

Free and considerate

Tabi is free. While your phone lies still, the app does not drain the battery, and it does not take your photos.
